    • What is drifting?
    Drifting is the art of driving a car through a corner while intentionally oversteering, causing the rear wheels to lose traction and slide sideways. It requires skill, precision, and control to maintain the drift while navigating the turn. Drifting is often associated with motorsports and has gained popularity in car culture worldwide.
